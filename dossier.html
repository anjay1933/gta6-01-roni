FROSTLINE

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME · FROSTLINE PASS · MAKE GTA 6 (GTA6-01)

You are Mara Voss, getaway driver for the Whiteout Crew. Frostline Pass is snowed in, the aurora is out, and the ski patrol watches every road. Run six jobs for Vera — from a quiet pickup at the lodge to a whiteout escape down the mountain — steal what you need, outrun the law, and get paid.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D — move / drive
Mouse — look / aim (click to lock)
Shift — sprint
Space — jump / handbrake
E — enter / exit car
Click — shoot  ·  F — punch
R — drive-by while driving
G — respray at the garage ($150, clears wanted)
P / Esc — pause  ·  M — mute

Stealing a car, hitting a pedestrian, or firing near patrols raises your wanted level (1–5 stars). Lay low until the heat cools, or pay $150 at the Snowline Garage. Ice is slick — brake early on the lake. Progress (cash, missions, health, armor, ammo) saves automatically.

WHAT'S IN THE WORLD

Main Street shops and a diner, the Frostline Lodge, seven cabins, a working chairlift, a frozen lake with an ice-fishing hut, pine forests, an icy mountain pass road, traffic cars and strolling pedestrians, patrol SUVs with roadblocks, health / armor / ammo pickups, a live minimap, and fully synthesized WebAudio (engine, wind, sirens, gunshots, music). No network requests — Three.js is vendored and everything else is generated in code.
